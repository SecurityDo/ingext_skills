# 2026-10-28 — mssp-coverage-map 1.0.0 (new)

| Skill | Version | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| mssp-coverage-map | 1.0.0 | released |

**Which customers' endpoint protection covers their people.** For every tenant a provider can act on, the
skill compares licensed Microsoft 365 seats with the endpoints and users in the tenant's EDR dumps, flags
the gap and ranks the under-covered tenants. It writes an HTML map and a CSV.

- **Inputs:** `list_accounts` for the roster, one `list_resource_dumps` per account, and the
  `office365User`, `falconAgent`, `sentinelOneAgent` and `msDefenderMachine` dumps of the accounts that
  have them, downloaded to disk. `scripts/plan_downloads.py` lists what to fetch and
  `scripts/build_report.py` computes everything.
- **Seats and endpoints:** a seat is an enabled Member with at least one assigned license; Falcon records,
  SentinelOne agents that are not decommissioned or uninstalled, and Onboarded Defender machines are the
  hosts; EDR users are the distinct last-login users. The gap is seats minus EDR users.
- **Beyond Falcon:** SentinelOne and Defender are read as well, so a tenant on either EDR is not shown as
  endpoint blind.
- **Honest limits:** the gap is directional; a dump that is listed but not downloaded shows as not
  collected, never as zero; an account with several customers for a resource is summed; the dumps are
  snapshots with their own times.
- First run: six tenants on the Develop connector, checked against the downloaded dumps.
