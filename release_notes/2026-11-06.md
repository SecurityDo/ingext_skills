## ransomware-readiness-report

- Resource data is downloaded from resource dumps and parsed locally; event data comes only from archived reports (Windows server activity and Entra sign-in activity). Nothing is queried live.
- CrowdStrike Falcon is supported alongside SentinelOne; the agent inventories are merged. A CrowdStrike-only tenant shows backup software, remote tools, threats and detect-only mode as visibility gaps.
- Ratings are rule-based and documented in `references/rating_thresholds.md`. Endpoint and threat severities follow the `sentinelOneConfig` and `falconAgentSecurity` reviews; the other thresholds are Fluency proposals.
- Conditional access is evaluated from the policy definitions (scope, exclusions, grant controls), not from per-user MFA registration.
- Known limit: recovery ability (backup success, age, immutability, restore tests) is not measurable, so backup exposure is the only backup signal.

## New: cyber-risk-in-dollars-report (R01)

- Board report from the library (R01): expected annual loss, 1-in-10 and 1-in-20 year loss, loss exceedance curve, quantified risk register and the dollar value of each recommended action. HTML and PDF in the editorial style.
- Scenarios: ransomware, business email compromise and cloud data breach, calibrated by fixed rules from the ransomware-readiness evidence and the archived sign-in report; missing data widens the range. Accidental and insider exposure (shadow AI) is not modelled until a DLP or generative-AI source exists.
- Optional intake (revenue or downtime cost, insurance limit, risk appetite, control costs); without it loss is scaled from a reference organization and the report says so.
