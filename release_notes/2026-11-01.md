# 2026-11-01 — iso-cmmc-compliance-analysis 1.0.0 (new)

| Skill | Version | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| iso-cmmc-compliance-analysis | 1.0.0 | released |

**The 800-53 evidence in ISO 27001 and CMMC language.** Reads the `compliance_summary.json` that
compliance-assessment writes and renders ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A and CMMC 2.0 Level 2 tables as HTML and
CSV. Prerequisite: compliance-assessment.

- **Contents:** each companion control with its related 800-53 groups and their statuses, a caveat, the
  strongest mapped areas, the not-observed areas and the evidence still needed.
- **Honest limits:** rows are related, not equivalent; statuses are never raised; the crosswalk is the
  analyst's and indicative; ISO management-system clauses are not covered; not a certification, a CMMC
  result or an attestation.
- First run: one test tenant's saved compliance-assessment result.
