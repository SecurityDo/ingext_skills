| Skill | Version | Status |

| mssp-ops-sitrep | 1.0.0 | released |

**What needs doing across my customers right now.** A read-only shift-handoff page for the accounts of a
connector: a summary, product posture for Office365, CrowdStrike Falcon and SentinelOne, and four lanes
(SOC Manager, Administrator, Operator, Investigator).

- **Inputs:** `list_accounts` and one `behavior_summary_search` per account for the window (score 3,000 or
  more). Optional: health-status-report results for the Administrator lane and product posture, and
  mssp-coverage-map results for identity-versus-endpoint coverage.
- **Queue meaning:** unworked is status new with no comment; escalated is status new after the AI assistant
  commented; ages run from `incidentDetectionTime`.
- **Honest limits:** missing evidence shows as not collected, never zero; no state is stored, so the
  companion app's investigation-gap ledger has no equivalent; log flow and EDR agent health are not seen
  unless health-status-report ran in full mode.
- First run: two Conekt accounts for the quiet week, plus a scratch copy with tickets reopened to exercise
  the open and escalated paths.
