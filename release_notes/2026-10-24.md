| fluency-report-rollup | 1.0.1 | released |

**Archive timezone changes.** From platform 9.0.14 a tenant can move a report's archive to its
local timezone after days are already archived in UTC. The earlier days keep their own day
boundaries and still count. `coverage.timezones` then lists both zones, and the skill says on
the page that a few hours around the change are missing or counted twice.
