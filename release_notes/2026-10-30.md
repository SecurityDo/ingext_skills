# 2026-10-30 — office365-posture-review 1.0.0 (new)

| Skill | Version | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| office365-posture-review | 1.0.0 | released |

**Office 365 hygiene findings from the resource dumps.** Reviews users, application registrations and
devices of one tenant and ranks the findings by severity. It writes an HTML page and a CSV.

- **Inputs:** `list_resource_dumps`, then the `office365User`, `office365Application` and
  `office365Device` dumps downloaded to disk. No FPL report and no event query.
- **Checks:** privileged roles, weak MFA factors, OAuth-connected users, token baselines older than a
  year, password policy None; HTTP redirects, implicit grant, HTTP homepage, no verified publisher, no
  credentials, apps older than two years with risky settings; stale, Workplace-trust, unmanaged,
  non-compliant, rooted, stale-hybrid-sync and unattributed devices.
- **Compound risk:** privileged accounts that also have weak MFA, a stale token baseline or password
  policy None are called out.
- **Honest limits:** a check whose field is absent from the dump shows n/a, not zero; each Office 365
  feed is assessed on its own; counts are as of the dump time; resource posture is not activity.
- First run: one small test tenant, checked against the dump fields.
