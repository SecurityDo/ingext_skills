# 2026-10-26 — proofpoint-investigation 1.0.0 (new)

| Skill | Version | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| proofpoint-investigation | 1.0.0 | released |

**What really happened to one Proofpoint email.** The verdict is computed from the Proofpoint
event rows: sender, recipients (internal or external), direction, delivery, clicks, threats,
attachments, blast radius and routing. It combines the two companion skills that interpret a
Proofpoint case and answer follow-up questions.

- **Inputs:** `list_data_tables` (the Proofpoint table must be listed), `kql_search` over the
  Proofpoint table for the message, optional related messages and clicks, and optionally
  `behavior_summary_search` for the case context. `scripts/proofpoint_verdict.py` reads the saved
  results.
- **Direction from the participants.** An internal sender with external recipients is outbound
  even on an inbound cluster; the contradiction is reported, not followed.
- **Local user pivot.** When the case key is external but an internal address sent the message,
  the internal sender is the user to investigate.
- **Traps handled:** exact duplicate rows are removed and counted; a message both delivered and
  blocked is reported as mixed; a threat time more than 30 days before the message is flagged as a
  reused indicator; no click events means "no evidence", not "did not click".
- **Raw rows hold customer addresses and subjects.** The skill keeps them out of the repository
  and out of the report.
- First run: a delivered message with a threat attached on a Develop tenant, checked against the
  event fields.
- **Beyond the gateway:** a threat baseline (is the threat id common on the tenant), a sender and
  sender-IP query for related mail, delivery judged per recipient, and a post-delivery Office365
  check for each internal recipient that reuses the incident-investigation `bec_sweep.kql`.
