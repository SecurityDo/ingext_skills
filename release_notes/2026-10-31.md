# 2026-10-31 — nist-compliance-analysis 1.0.0 (new)

| Skill | Version | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| nist-compliance-analysis | 1.0.0 | released |

**What the evidence says, control by control.** Reads the `compliance_summary.json` that compliance-assessment
writes and renders a NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 analysis as HTML and CSV. Prerequisite: compliance-assessment.

- **Contents:** scope and framework, counts by status, a control table with evidence state and the next
  evidence to collect, and the highest-value evidence to gather.
- **Child views:** an optional CSF 2.0 and SP 800-171 Rev. 3 view at category level, labelled as the
  analyst's indicative crosswalk, with the 800-53 control kept as the lineage.
- **Honest limits:** statuses are used as returned; not observed is never shown as a failure; nothing is
  collected or invented; the output is not a certification or attestation.
- First run: one test tenant's saved compliance-assessment result.
